World Modeling
in Transformers

Pierre Beckmann · Matthieu Queloz · André Freitas

A transformer can learn a faithful map and still lose its way. We study how TaxiGPT represents Manhattan, locates itself, and uses its map to navigate—and what happens when these mechanisms fail.
