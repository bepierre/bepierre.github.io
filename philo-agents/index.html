What happens at review?

  1. PrepareTarget and evidence
  2. BuildArgument v1
  3. ChallengeObjections to v1
  4. AssessArgument v1 + objections
Use cleared→ Integrate within the allowed scope
Repair needed↶ Propose a correction → prepare again → assess the new version
Evidence missing→ Return to the responsible earlier stage or pause

The critic names what the exact version can be used for. A changed version needs a fresh assessment. Plans and reports can also go through their own review loops.

Who can open a smaller question?

The optional controller belongs to the inquiry dossier. A due checkpoint can pause new exploratory assignments across the stages above. An operator or host must get a branching decision admitted under the dossier’s policy.

  1. CheckpointContinue, branch, return, or pause
  2. Admit a childQuestion, call budget, and what to bring back
  3. Run child workEligible work package or controlled reasoning
  4. Return to parentExact result and review status
